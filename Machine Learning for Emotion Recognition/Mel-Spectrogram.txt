Mel-Spectrogram features in speech audio usually consist of 80 or 128 mel frequency bands (or "bins") arranged vertically, representing frequency, while the horizontal axis represents time frames. The specific number of bands is a hyperparameter chosen based on the task, typically ranging from 50 to 130 to balance spectral resolution with computational efficiency, according to a Medium article.
